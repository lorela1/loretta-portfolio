Bast

A hieroglyph-wordsearch visual novel about a reincarnated temple cat

Overview

Bast is a 10-minute vertical slice built solo over 12 weeks: the player is reincarnated as a temple cat trying to communicate with their grieving family after dying as an Ancient Egyptian priestess-princess, solving hieroglyph wordsearch puzzles to advance branching dialogue toward one of sixteen endings.

Design & production

  • Chose the visual novel format deliberately: it played to writing and Unity strengths while keeping the programming scope achievable solo in 12 weeks.
  • Planned the production with a Gantt chart and Trello board before writing a line of code, then wrote the full plot and dialogue in a Game Design Document.
  • Hit a hard technical blocker when the PixelCrusher dialogue system couldn't parse hieroglyph characters; rather than keep fighting the tool, switched to YarnSpinner mid-production after weighing the tradeoffs.
  • Used AI tools (ChatGPT for art, Claude for code, Suno for music) deliberately as placeholder-asset generators to keep the 12-week prototype on schedule, treating their output as raw material to verify and rework rather than final content.
  • Ran continuous playtesting and iterated directly on the feedback: added a timed puzzle-scramble mechanic and a story-hook opening scene after testers found the wordsearch too static and the narrative too slow to grab attention.
  • Wrote a full production postmortem covering technical decision-making and lessons for future solo prototypes.
Role

Solo Developer (Design, Writing & Programming)

Tools

Unity, YarnSpinner, Text Mesh Pro
ChatGPT, Claude & Suno (placeholder assets)

Scran Dash

A cozy hedgehog-dad game, crossing Katamari Damacy with Untitled Goose Game

Overview

Scran Dash is a cozy game about a hedgehog dad rolling up into a ball to gather food and items for his hoglets, denned in the mausoleum of a buried magic-user whose spellbook he's inherited. I pitched the concept — a whimsical cross between Katamari Damacy and Untitled Goose Game, set on the grounds of a gothic mansion — and it's the idea that won me the Design Lead role on the team.

Design & leadership

  • Originated the core concept and pitched it to the team; the rolling pickup mechanic it was built around is what won the team over.
  • As Design Lead, wrote the majority of the Game Design Document, especially the setting, narrative, backstory and quests.
  • Allocated work by individual strength: Kieran on the resource-management game loop, Virginia on UI and player movement/pickup, Megan on game art.
  • Replanned scope after one team member fell ill and another dropped out entirely — downscoping the resource-management loop to a prototype that nods to the fuller system planned for future iterations, protecting the project deadline.
  • Ran weekly scrums and used Codecks to keep the team aligned between meetings, with GitHub for version control across a AAA-style production brief.
Role

Design Lead & Ideator

Team

Virginia — UI, Movement & Pickup
Megan — Game Art
Kieran — Resource Management (departed mid-project)
