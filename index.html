About

I'm a game designer with a BA in Games Design  (2023–2026). I've written the design documents, run the sprints and led the teams on three productions: a cozy detective game jam (Doggo Detective), a horror-stealth game jam (Little Pig), and a twenty-person academic AAA capstone (Sinner's Belt). I was appointed Lead Designer in all of these productions.

Before that, I spent close to a decade in legal advocacy and research — at Chambers and Partners, the Free Representation Unit, the Access to Justice Foundation, the Communities Empowerment Network and Citizens Advice — representing clients, drafting arguments, and managing casework under fixed deadlines.

As a producer

Evidence from three productions, not a list of buzzwords.

Team structure

Appointed Lead Designer on Sinner's Belt, a 20-person production split across two campuses.  Directed the multi-disciplinary team, making resourcing decisions and scope evaluations

Schedules & milestones

Ran weekly sprint reviews and kept sprint logs across all three productions, tracking scope against a fixed deadlines using industry standard software and methods like Agile and Trello. 

Scope cuts

Downscoped Little Pig from three rooms to two, mid-production, to ship a complete demo instead of an unfinished larger one.

Playtest → iteration

Took Doggo Detective's prototype to external testers, then rewrote the tutorial and added a non-colour visual cue after players missed the core puzzle.

Postmortems

Wrote structured postmortems after each production — Doggo Detective, Little Pig and Sinner's Belt — and changed process on the next project as a result.

Conflict resolution

Arbitrated a texturing dispute between two artists on Sinner's Belt as Lead Designer, and repaired a workload misunderstanding with a programmer on Little Pig through direct conversation.

As a game designer

Design decisions from two self-initiated projects, where the design was entirely mine to originate and defend.

Originating a concept

Pitched Scran Dash's core idea — a hedgehog dad rolling into a ball, crossing Katamari Damacy with Untitled Goose Game — and won the Design Lead role on the strength of it.

Systemic puzzle design

Built Bast's core loop around hieroglyph wordsearch puzzles gating branching dialogue toward one of sixteen endings.

Worldbuilding & narrative

Wrote Bast's full story and dialogue — a reincarnated temple cat trying to reach a grieving family — and Scran Dash's setting and backstory as Design Lead.

Development Pipeline Problem Solving

Switched Bast to another branching dialogue tool -- YarnSpinner --  mid-production after the original dialogue system couldn't parse hieroglyph characters.

Playtest-driven iteration

Added a timed puzzle-scramble mechanic and a story-hook opening to Bast after testers found the wordsearch too static and the narrative too slow to grab attention.

Scoping under constraint

Replanned Scran Dash's resource-management loop into a smaller working prototype after one team members fell ill and another dropped out mid-project.
