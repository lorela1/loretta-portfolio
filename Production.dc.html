Case studies

Production Portfolio

Three group productions, most recent first: a 20-person academic AAA capstone split across two campuses and two mid-size collaborations with 12 week runways. Each below entry covers the design, the production decisions, and what leading the team actually looked like week to week. Solo and designer work lives on the Design Portfolio page.

Academic Capstone · 10 Weeks · Sep–Dec 2025 · ~20-person team, two campuses

Sinner's Belt

A dystopian sci-fi FPS built across two campuses, produced to an AAA brief

Overview

I was appointed Lead Designer project, as well as absorbing the production-manager role after the Head of Production left the team mid-project.

Production & leadership

  • Managed a four-week cross-campus design conflict over role boundaries; when it couldn't be resolved, the two offices split to build separate versions of the game, and I fed structured feedback into the process afterward.
  • Arbitrated a texturing dispute between the level designer and art lead as Lead Designer — resolved quickly because the team's decision hierarchy was already established.
  • Ran a playtesting survey across the vertical slice and revised its language after a tester flagged jargon.
  • Designed the onboarding tutorial sequence — my biggest individual contribution to the shipped slice — and led the team postmortem afterward.
Role

Lead Designer (Quest & Level Design)

Game Jam · 11 Weeks · May–Jul 2025 · Team of 6

Little Pig

A horror-stealth puzzle platformer about a pig escaping the slaughterhouse

Overview

Little Pig combines horror and animals: a pig escaping an abattoir, standing on two legs to interact with the environment.  I ran the design collaboratively from the start, scoping the concept for a 10-week production window.

Production & leadership

  • Split level design work with co-lead Megan — she designed one room, I designed the other — while supporting her research as well as holding mechanic design ownership.
  • Mediated an art-pipeline dispute between the co-lead and the character artist, and agreed to source a backup outside artist for the antagonist model to protect the schedule.
  • Resolved a workload miscommunication with the team's programmer over implementation responsibility through direct conversation rather than letting it fester.
  • Jointly made the call to downscope from three rooms to two partway through production, to protect the final milestone.
  • Wrote the itch.io store copy, designed the external playtest questionnaire, and ran the team postmortem — the biggest process fixes identified were earlier Git adoption and an earlier first playable.
Role

Co-Lead Designer, Narrative Design & QA Lead

Game Jam · 12 Weeks · May–Aug 2024 · Team of 8

Doggo Detective

A cozy detective game about a Hong Kong police dog

Overview

Pitched from research into Hong Kong police films and cozy animal games (Untitled Goose Game, Stray), Doggo Detective casts the player as a Hong Kong police dog solving petty crimes through handwriting analysis and photography. I was appointed Lead Designer wrote the pitch and the Game Design Document.

Production & leadership

  • Ran weekly scrum meetings, deputising for the producer when he became unavailable, keeping all the scrum logs up to date. 
  • Managed a team member who repeatedly missed deliverables by reassigning them to QA, with a backup programmer covering the work in parallel as a hedge.
  • Took the prototype to external playtesters and iterated on the results: rewrote the tutorial text and added a colour-independent particle cue after testers missed the core puzzle.
  • Wrote a full team postmortem covering communication gaps in the programming sub-team and inconsistent use of the Trello board.
Role

Game Designer & Team Leader
